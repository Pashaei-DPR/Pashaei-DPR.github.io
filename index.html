About Me

I am a researcher focusing on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, emergent coordination, complex adaptive systems, and RL for sequential decision-making in partially observable environments.

Education

  • M.Sc. in Sports Engineering, University of Tehran (UT), Iran (2024-2026).
    • Thesis: Opponent Tactical Awareness in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Football.
  • B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (Mechatronics), Isfahan University of Technology (IUT), Iran (2018-2023).
    • Thesis: Audio-Based Fault Diagnosis of Ventilation Fans Using Machine Learning Algorithms.

Projects

  • Adaptive Automated Insulin Delivery (2026): Built a robust adaptive PID controller (EKF + ADMM) and benchmarked it against RL controllers (PPO, PPO-PID), achieving top time-in-range and zero hypoglycemia in virtual T1D/T2D trials.
  • Multi-Objective Fuzzy C-Means Posture Classification (2026): Used NSGA-II to optimize FCM thresholds, finding Pareto-optimal trade-offs between information preservation and cluster compactness.

Ongoing Projects

  • Strategic Alignment & Reward Optimization in Multi-Agent Systems (2026): Simulated multi-agent RL to study how cooperative behavior and effective rules emerge from environmental structure rather than top-down reward design.
  • MARL-Based Football Simulation Environment: Developed a MARL-based football simulation environment to study opponent tactics, decentralized coordination, and emergent behavior.
